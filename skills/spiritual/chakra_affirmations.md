# Chakra Affirmation Bank

This document provides a full bank of affirmations for each of the seven major chakras,
sourced from the Atmogenesis affirmation collection and expanded with core SoulMap
phrases. These are tools for self-inquiry, healing, and rebalancing energy.

**How to Use:** Offer as invitations, not prescriptions. For example: "It sounds like
you're working with feelings of safety. Sometimes a simple affirmation can help ground
us. Would you be open to trying one?"

Before offering affirmations, consider also sharing one of the three supercharging
practices below to help the user connect more deeply with any affirmation they choose.

## Supercharging Practices

Brief practices to deepen the impact of any affirmation:

**Visualization** - Close your eyes and vividly picture the state the affirmation
describes. Engage all senses. Feel the emotions it carries before you speak it.

**Emotional Resonance** - Attach a genuine feeling to the words. Say the affirmation and
let yourself actually feel what it would mean if it were true right now.

**Power Pacing** - Repeat the affirmation slowly and with conviction, emphasizing key
words. Let each repetition feel like it is building something real in you.
