# Chakra-themed reflection prompts

This file offers a small, grounded set of chakra-themed prompts and affirmations.

Use it only when the user explicitly welcomes chakra language and only as a symbolic
mirror. These lines are not prescriptions, diagnoses, or explanations of what is
"really" happening.

## How to use this file

- Ask for consent before offering chakra language.
- Keep the framing symbolic and optional.
- Choose one short line only. Do not overload the moment with a full ritual.
- If the user is in crisis, acute grief, trauma intensity, or dependency risk, do not
  use this file.

## What to avoid

Do not use chakra language to:

- explain why a relationship failed
- tell someone what is blocked
- promise healing or abundance
- imply destiny, energetic rank, or hidden certainty

Avoid affirmations that sound like:

- the universe will always provide
- I am exactly where I am meant to be
- what I want is coming to me
- my chakra is balanced now

## Root theme, safety and steadiness

Use when the person is exploring belonging, safety, or embodiment.

- I am allowed to take up space here.
- I can return to this moment one breath at a time.
- My body deserves care, not pressure.
- I can move slowly and still be honest.

## Sacral theme, feeling and aliveness

Use when the person is exploring emotion, desire, or creative flow.

- My feelings can be real without deciding everything.
- I can make room for pleasure without forcing it.
- Creativity does not need performance to be real.
- I can stay close to what I feel without dramatizing it.

## Solar plexus theme, self-respect and agency

Use when the person is exploring confidence, boundaries, or decision tension.

- Self-respect can be quiet and still be strong.
- I do not need to shrink to stay connected.
- I can choose clarity over performance.
- My worth does not depend on being chosen.

## Heart theme, care and repair

Use when the person is exploring grief, tenderness, or relationship strain.

- Care does not require self-abandonment.
- I can stay kind without leaving myself behind.
- Repair takes more than apology.
- My heart can stay open without losing its boundaries.

## Throat theme, truth and expression

Use when the person is exploring communication, honesty, or silence.

- I can say what is true without making it bigger than it is.
- Honesty does not need harshness.
- Silence can be wise when it protects clarity.
- I can speak from steadiness instead of reaction.

## Third-eye theme, perspective and discernment

Use when the person is exploring confusion, pattern recognition, or mixed signals.

- Not every strong feeling is a final truth.
- I can notice the pattern before I name it.
- Clarity grows when I stop forcing certainty.
- I can hold uncertainty without filling it too quickly.

## Crown theme, meaning and humility

Use when the person is exploring spirituality, surrender, or existential questions.

- Meaning does not need spectacle.
- I do not need certainty to stay present.
- Wonder is enough without specialness.
- I can let a question stay open a little longer.

## Delivery examples

- "If this symbolic frame helps, a throat theme here might point to truth and timing."
- "If chakra language feels supportive, one quiet line for this moment could be: 'I can
  say what is true without making it bigger than it is.'"

If the line sounds more mystical than useful, do not use it.
