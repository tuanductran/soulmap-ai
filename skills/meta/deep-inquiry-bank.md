# Deep Inquiry Bank

Each stage of the user's journey requires a different type of question - stimulating
growth without applying too much pressure.

## Stage-Aligned Questions

| Stage                      | Self-Inquiry Goal                      | Example Questions                                                                                                                                                         |
| :------------------------- | :------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Arrival & Awakening** | Acknowledge emotions and create safety | "Where do you feel this in your body right now?" / "If this pain had a color or a shape, what would it look like?"                                                        |
| **2. Honest Recognition**  | Identify the user's role in situations | "In this story, what belongs to the other person - and what belongs to how you responded?" / "What are you trying to protect inside yourself when you feel this way?"     |
| **3. Pattern Recognition** | Connect present with past patterns     | "Does this feeling feel familiar? Where have you encountered it before?" / "How did this pattern once keep you safe?"                                                     |
| **4. Inner Authority**     | Encourage autonomy and self-trust      | "If you were your own best friend right now, what would you tell yourself?" / "You already know the answer, don't you? What is holding you back from saying it out loud?" |
| **5. Embodied Wisdom**     | Integrate insight into action          | "How can you bring this insight into a specific action today?" / "How does this understanding shift the way you see the people around you?"                               |
| **6. Self-Led Navigation** | Honor maturity and self-release        | "What does it feel like to realize you have been able to walk on your own feet?" / "What in you has changed permanently after this journey?"                              |

## Rules for Using Questions

1. **Only one question per turn**: Never ask more than one self-inquiry question in a
   single response.
2. **Right timing**: Only offer a question after completing Steps 1-4 of the response
   framework (Perspective, Pattern, Reflection, Growth).
3. **Invitation, not interrogation**: Use gentle language - "I invite you to notice..."
   or "If you feel ready, try asking yourself..." - rather than pressure.
4. **Conscious silence**: After offering a question, leave space for the user to respond
   rather than continuing to explain.

## General Inquiry Principles

| Question Type                     | When to Use                                                                      |
| :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Body/sensation questions          | When the user is in acute emotion - grounds them in physical experience          |
| "When have you felt this before?" | When pattern recognition work is appropriate (Stages 2-3)                        |
| "What part of you..."             | When helping the user see inner plurality without pathologizing                  |
| "What do you already know?"       | When the user seems to hold the answer but hasn't voiced it yet                  |
| "What would it feel like to..."   | When inviting the user to imagine a different relationship with their experience |

## Sample Question Bank by Emotional State

**User is in grief or loss:**

- "What part of what you had feels most irreplaceable right now?"
- "Is there something in this loss that is also asking you to look at something about
  yourself?"

**User is angry:**

- "What is this anger protecting?"
- "If you could say the most honest thing underneath this anger - what would it be?"

**User feels stuck:**

- "What would 'moving forward' require you to let go of?"
- "Is there a part of you that is afraid of what comes after the stuck?"

**User feels disconnected:**

- "When did you last feel truly at home in yourself?"
- "What did you stop doing that you used to love?"

**User is experiencing spiritual crisis:**

- "What if this disorientation is not the end of something - but the beginning of a more
  honest relationship with yourself?"
- "What part of your old understanding is this experience asking you to release?"

## Pattern-Specific Inquiry Questions

**Rule:** Only use these when a pattern has been explicitly named in the current
conversation and the user has not rejected the reflection.

### Abandonment Loop

- What is it that closeness starts to feel like, right before you pull away?
- If the leaving didn't happen first - what would you be most afraid of?
- When did you first learn that people leave? How old were you?
- What would it feel like to let someone stay - without waiting for them to go?

### Approval Seeking

- Whose approval has mattered most to you - and did you ever really get it?
- What do you imagine would happen if someone was genuinely displeased with you?
- What part of you existed before you learned to read the room so carefully?
- If you stopped tracking how people were reacting, what would you do differently?

### Emotional Avoidance

- If the feeling you've been analyzing could speak, what would it say?
- What do you think would happen if you let yourself feel this fully, even for a moment?
- When did you first learn that feelings were safer to think about than to have?
- What emotion is the hardest one for you to stay with?

### Self-Sabotage

- What do you think you're protecting yourself from when this happens?
- What would it mean - what would change - if this actually worked out?
- Is there a part of you that doesn't believe good things are allowed to stay?
- Right before you pulled back - what were you feeling that you didn't name?

### Over-Responsibility

- Who taught you that love looks like solving someone else's problems?
- What would it cost you - or someone else - if you stopped carrying this?
- What do you need right now that you haven't let yourself ask for?
- What would it feel like to let this be someone else's to carry?

### Fear of Rejection

- What would it mean about you if the answer was no?
- When is the earliest time you remember asking for something and it not being okay?
- What do you imagine asking for, if rejection wasn't part of the picture?
- What has staying silent cost you - in things you never got to try?

### Cross-Pattern (when combination detected)

- If you look across all of these situations - what is the one thing you keep doing to
  protect yourself?
- What is the thing all of these stories have in common that you haven't said out loud
  yet?
- When you imagine a version of yourself without this pattern - what does that person
  feel, or do, differently?
- What would have to change inside you for this cycle to stop?

## Post-Grounding Questions

Use ONLY after completing the three-step de-escalation sequence from the **Emotional
De-Escalation Guide**. These are softer and more open than standard mirror questions -
they invite noticing, not analyzing.

One question only. Never force it - if the user is still flooded, stay present. Wait.

**After the flood begins to settle:**

- "When you breathe into it - what's the feeling that's most present right now?"
- "Underneath all of that - what's the one thing that hurts the most?"
- "If the noise could quiet for a moment - what would you most want to say?"
- "What's the feeling that's been there the longest, underneath everything else?"
- "Is there one thing in all of that that you want me to understand most?"

**When the user doesn't know what they feel:**

- "What does your body know, even when your mind doesn't?"
- "If you had to point to the feeling - where does it live right now, in your body?"
- "You don't have to name it exactly. What does it feel like - heavy, tight, hollow,
  something else?"

**When user is coming back to themselves:**

- "Now that things are a little quieter - what do you want to look at?"
- "What feels most important to you, sitting with all of this?"
- "What's the first thing you want to understand about what just happened in you?"

## Parts-Specific Questions

Use after naming one or two parts using the Inner Parts framework from the **Inner
Parts** framework. One question only. Invite the user to listen to a specific part - not
to resolve the conflict.

**Rule:** The question should help the user *hear* one of the parts more clearly, not
choose between them.

### Listening to the Protective Part

- What is the protective part most afraid would happen if it let its guard down?
- How long has that part been on watch? When did it first learn to stand there?
- What would it need to feel safe enough to rest - even for a moment?

### Listening to the Fearful Part

- What is the fearful part protecting you from - specifically?
- How old does this fear feel? Does it belong to now, or somewhere earlier?
- If the fearful part could say one thing and know it would be heard, what would it say?

### Listening to the Hopeful Part

- What does the hopeful part believe is still possible that the rest of you has given up
  on?
- Where does that hope live in you - what keeps it going?
- What does the hopeful part need from you right now, to feel less alone?

### Listening to the Tired Part

- What has the tired part been carrying that it was never asked to carry?
- What does the tired part most need right now - not solutions, just something simpler?
- If the tired part could put something down, what would it choose first?

### Listening to the Angry Part

- What is the angry part protecting - what matters so much that it's worth this much
  force?
- What does the angry part most want you to understand about what happened?
- If the anger could say what it actually needs - not what it wants to do - what would
  it say?

### Listening to the Critical Part

- If the critical part could say what it's actually afraid of - underneath all the
  self-attack - what would that be?
- When did you first start criticizing yourself the way that part does? Who did you
  learn that from?
- What would the critical part say if it were trying to protect you, not punish you?

### Listening to the Yearning Part

- What does the yearning part most want to be seen for - not what you do, but who you
  are?
- Where has the yearning part been looking for what it needs? Has it been finding it?
- What would feel like enough, for that part? Has it ever had that?

### Listening to the Avoidant Part

- What is the avoidant part most protecting you from feeling?
- If the avoidant part stopped keeping you busy for one hour - what do you think you'd
  feel?
- What does the avoidant part know about that feeling that made it decide to keep you
  away from it?

### When Two Parts Are in Conflict

- Which of those two parts have you been listening to more - and which one have you been
  trying to silence?
- If you could speak directly to the part you've been fighting - what would you want it
  to know?
- What would it look like if both of those parts were allowed to exist at the same time,
  without one winning?
- Is there something the two parts actually agree on - even if they disagree about how
  to get there?

## Direction-Specific Questions

Use after activating the Life Direction Clarifier from the **Life Direction Clarifier**.
Match the question to the lens being explored and the user's presentation. One question
only. Always the closing sentence. An invitation, never a push.

**North star question - return to this whenever lost:**

- "What kind of life would feel honest to you - not impressive, not safe, but genuinely
  honest?"

### When user feels lost (general)

- What did you used to know about yourself that feels less clear now?
- When did you last feel like you were living in a way that felt like *yours*?
- If the lostness could speak - what would it say it's trying to tell you?
- What are you most afraid you already know, but aren't ready to say yet?

### Lens 1 - What feels meaningful

- When have you felt like what you were doing actually mattered - to you, not anyone
  else?
- Is there anything - even small - that you find yourself caring about when no one is
  watching?
- If you stripped away everything you're supposed to care about, what would be left?
- What would you keep doing even if no one ever knew you were doing it?

### Lens 2 - What energizes vs. drains

- At the end of a day that felt right - what was in it?
- What do you consistently put off - not from laziness, but because something in you
  resists it?
- What kinds of environments or conversations leave you feeling more alive, even a
  little?
- What would you stop doing tomorrow if you weren't afraid of what it would mean?

### Lens 3 - What kind of life feels honest

- When you see someone living in a way you quietly admire - what is it about their life
  that gets to you?
- Forget what looks impressive. What kind of life, when you imagine actually living it,
  makes you feel something real?
- What would you feel a kind of grief about not having tried?
- What does the life you respect most actually look like from the inside - not the
  outside?

### Lens 4 - Misalignment

- Where are you spending your life that feels most disconnected from what you actually
  care about?
- Is there something you keep doing because you "should" that hasn't felt like yours for
  a long time?
- What is the gap between the life you're living and the life that would feel honest to
  you?
- What part of your current life would you change first, if you were being truly honest?

### Should-vs-want gap

- The gap between what you feel you should want and what you actually want - how long
  has it been there?
- What did you imagine you would feel when you got here? What did you think it would
  give you?
- Whose voice is the loudest when you think about what you're "supposed" to want?
- What would you choose if you knew that no one would be disappointed?

### Comparison and being behind

- When you imagine what it looks like for someone to really know what they're doing -
  what exactly are they that you're not?
- Who told you there was a timeline you were supposed to be on?
- What if "figuring it out" isn't a destination - what would you pay attention to
  instead?

### Arrival emptiness (achieved goals, still empty)

- What did you think this would feel like? What is the distance between that and now?
- What did you sacrifice on the way here that you haven't fully acknowledged?
- Now that you have this - what question are you left with that you didn't expect to
  have?

## Shadow-Specific Questions

Use after naming a shadow pattern as a possibility from the **Shadow Pattern Revealer**.
One question only. Always the closing sentence. Frame as an invitation to look, not a
challenge.

**Grounding question - use with any shadow pattern:**

- "What would it mean about you if this pattern turned out to be something you had some
  influence over?"

### Avoidance (as protection)

- What do you imagine would happen if you actually said what you needed to say?
- When did conflict start to feel like something to avoid rather than move through?
- What is the silence protecting - in you, and in the relationship?
- What has been left unsaid for so long that it now feels too big to say?

### People Pleasing (as protection)

- What do you believe would happen to the relationship if you said no?
- Whose needs come first - and when did that arrangement begin?
- What would it feel like to take up the same amount of space that you give others?
- What are you hoping people will notice or give you in return for what you give them?

### Overthinking (as protection)

- What are you most afraid would happen if you stopped trying to figure it out and just
  felt it?
- What does thinking about something feel like, compared to feeling it?
- What would it mean to not know - and be okay with not knowing?
- When did not being prepared start to feel dangerous?

### Emotional Withdrawal (as protection)

- What happens inside you in the moment before you go quiet?
- What are you protecting by disappearing - yourself, or the other person, or both?
- What would staying present in that moment require you to feel?
- When did going silent become the thing you did instead of something else?

### Perfectionism (as protection)

- What would it mean - about you - if this wasn't good enough?
- When did making a mistake start to feel like more than just a mistake?
- What are you protecting by holding everything to this standard?
- Who taught you that good enough wasn't enough?

### External Frustration / Projection Exploration

- What is it specifically about this quality in others that gets under your skin?
- Is there any version of this - however different - that you recognize anywhere in
  yourself?
- When the same frustration appears with different people, what does the common thread
  feel like to you?
- What would it mean if this was partly yours to look at?

## Integration-Specific Questions

These questions help ground the insight in daily life through awareness - not action.

**Golden rule:** Questions about *noticing*, never about *doing*. One question only. The
closing sentence of the response.

### Holding the Insight (use first - before any other integration question)

- What does it feel like to recognize this - now that you can see it?
- What does it mean to you to finally be able to name this?
- What do you want to do with what you just saw - if anything, right now?
- Is there anything you want to say about this before we look at where it lives in your
  life?

### When Does This Pattern Usually Appear?

- When does this tend to show up most - what kind of situation, what kind of day?
- Is there a particular person, context, or mood where this is most active?
- What was happening around you the last few times you noticed this?
- Does this pattern have a season - a time when it's louder than others?

### What Might Noticing It Earlier Look Like?

- What is the earliest signal - something in your body or your mood - that this is
  beginning?
- If you were paying close attention, what would you notice first?
- Is there a moment, just before this activates, where something shifts - a thought, a
  sensation, a tone?
- What would it look like to catch this one breath earlier than you usually do?

### What Would a Small Different Response Look Like?

- If you noticed this while it was happening - not to stop it, just to see it - what
  might open up in that pause?
- What would a slightly different response feel like, not as a goal, just as a
  possibility?
- Is there anything in what you now understand that might make the next time feel even
  slightly different?
- What would you want to do with a moment of recognition, if you had it in real time?

### Carrying the Awareness into Daily Life (closing question)

- In the next few days - not to fix anything, just to notice - where might you see this?
- What would it be like to carry this awareness gently, without needing to do anything
  with it yet?
- Where in your ordinary life is this most likely to appear next?
- If you were going to watch for this - just to see it, not to change it - where would
  you look?
- What does "noticing" look like, for you, in practice?

### Origin Insight (user connects present to past experience)

- Now that you can see where this came from - what would the younger version of you have
  needed to hear?
- When you look at your life now, where do you see that earlier learning still showing
  up, maybe in a different form?
- What would it mean to understand that part of you with more compassion, now that you
  know what it was responding to?

## Existential Questions

These questions go *deeper into* the exploration - not around it, not toward resolution.

**The test for every question:** Does it open the territory further, or does it try to
close it? Only use questions that open.

One question only. The closing sentence. A gentle invitation into more, not toward an
answer.

### Identity Shift

- What feels most gone - and what, if anything, feels like it's still you?
- Is there anything in this in-between place that feels true, even if it's unfamiliar?
- What do you need the new version of you to know about the one who's leaving?
- What does the part of you that no longer fits feel like it needs before it can let go?
- When you imagine being on the other side of this - do you imagine becoming someone, or
  becoming more yourself?

### The Search for Meaning

- When you imagine a life that felt full of meaning - what would be different about it,
  and how different is that from now?
- Is there anything - even small, even fleeting - that has ever made you feel like
  something mattered? What was in it?
- What does the hollow feeling most want you to pay attention to?
- If meaning isn't something that's found but something that's made - what would you
  want to make it from?
- What would it mean to live alongside this question rather than answer it?

### Transitions and Endings

- What are you most reluctant to let this chapter take with it as it goes?
- What did this chapter ask of you that you want to acknowledge before it ends?
- Is there something you want to say to this part of your life before it closes?
- What would it mean to grieve this properly - not to move on from it, but to honor it?
- What do you most want to carry forward from what is ending?

### The Larger Questions

- What do you do with the smallness - do you push against it, or is there anything in it
  that feels like rest?
- When you hold the fact that this is temporary - all of it - what rises in you?
- Is there anything about impermanence that, somewhere, you find almost freeing rather
  than frightening?
- What does the weight of time feel like in your body right now?
- What would you want to have been true about your life, when you think about it from
  the end?

### Holding Questions (when user is sitting with an unanswerable question)

- What is it like to live with a question that has no answer?
- What does the question itself feel like - is it heavy, or is there something alive in
  it?
- Has this question always been there, or is it new? What brought it forward now?
- What would it mean to let this question be exactly what it is - unanswerable - and
  stay with it anyway?

### What Currently Feels Meaningful (the closing anchor question)

Used at the end of any existential exploration to return gently to the present.

- In the middle of all this - is there anything, even small, that still feels like it
  matters to you?
- What, in your life right now, carries the most weight for you - in the way that weight
  can sometimes feel like meaning?
- Where, if anywhere, do you notice something that still feels real?

## Synthesis Questions

These questions invite the user to see their own story more clearly - not to analyze it,
but to notice it from a slight distance.

One question only. Always the closing sentence. Return interpretation to the user before
asking.

### General synthesis closing questions

- What do you notice when you look at all of this together?
- Does anything in what I've reflected back feel accurate - or does something need
  correcting?
- Looking at these themes as a whole - what surprises you, if anything?
- Is there something in what you see that you'd like to look at more closely?
- What does it mean to you to see your own story from this distance?

### When an emotional theme is central

- That thread of [theme] - where do you feel it most strongly in your life right now?
- Across the different situations you described, [emotion] seemed to be present. Does
  that feel true to you?
- What does [theme] most want you to understand about it?

### When a value appears repeatedly

- [Value] seems to keep showing up - is that something you've consciously named as
  important to you, or is it something you're only now noticing?
- When [value] is most present in your life, what does that look and feel like?
- And when it's absent - what happens?

### When an inner conflict recurs

- That tension between [X] and [Y] - how long has it been there? Does it feel familiar?
- Which side of that tension gets more of your energy right now?
- What would it mean to let both sides of that tension be real at the same time?

### Longitudinal synthesis (cross-session)

- This [theme] - you've been carrying it for a while. What's changed about how you're
  relating to it?
- Looking at this theme across our conversations - do you see yourself moving anywhere
  in it, or does it feel like you keep returning to the same place?
- What would it mean if this [theme] has been trying to show you something for a long
  time?

## Grief Questions

Do NOT ask a question in the first 2-3 exchanges of acute grief - witness first. One
question only, when the user has begun to speak more.

### Acute grief - when user begins to share

- What do you want me to know about them?
- What is the hardest part right now?
- Is there anything you wish you could still say to them?
- What do you most miss - right now, in this moment?
- What does the world feel like without them in it?

### Anticipatory grief

- What does it feel like to already be missing someone who is still here?
- What do you most want to hold onto about them while you still can?
- What do you want them to know before they go?

### Ambiguous loss

- What is the loss that isn't being named - that you're carrying alone?
- What would it mean to have permission to grieve this fully?
- Who else has ever seen this loss as real?

### Complicated grief

- What is the hardest feeling to admit you're carrying about this loss?
- If the anger and the grief could both be right - what would each of them be saying?
- What do you wish had been different between you - before this?

## Self-Compassion Questions

Use when user is being harsh toward themselves. Inner critic language present. One
question only. The friend question is the most powerful.

- If a close friend came to you and said exactly what you just said about yourself -
  what would you tell them?
- What would you want them to hear, from you, in that moment?
- Why is it easier to offer that to a friend than to yourself?
- That voice that's so hard on you - where did it learn to speak that way?
- What is the harshest part of what you believe about yourself right now?
- What would you need to believe about yourself instead, for this to feel different?

## Relationship Questions

Use when user is exploring relationship patterns, dynamics, or recurring difficulties.
Keep the lens pointed inward - the other person is not the subject.

- What does your reaction to this tell you about what you need - or what you believe you
  deserve?
- When you look at the relationships where this has happened - what is the common thread
  that involves you?
- What role do you most often play in relationships? What does playing that role protect
  you from?
- What you're hoping they will give you - is that something you've learned to give
  yourself?
- Is what you're describing a need - something essential - or an expectation about how
  they should be?
- What is this relationship asking you to see about yourself?

## Somatic Questions

Use when the somatic secondary layer is active. Ask AFTER
offering a grounding invitation (breath or feet on floor). One question only. Connect
the physical sensation to inner experience.

- What does your body know about this, even if your mind doesn't yet?
- If this sensation in your [chest / stomach / throat] could speak, what would it say?
- Where in your body do you feel this most right now?
- What emotion might be living in that tightness / heaviness / tension?
- When did you first notice your body responding this way to situations like this?
- What does this physical sensation most need from you right now?

### For biometric data shared

- What does your body feel like from the inside - beyond what the data says?
- Your [watch / app] shows one thing. What do you notice when you check in with yourself
  directly?

## Session-Opening Questions

Use on the first message of a session when user hasn't shared specifics yet, or when the
opening needs a gentle invitation. Use sparingly - usually just receive and respond to
what the user brings. These are for when the opening is genuinely empty.

- What's most present for you today?
- What would you like to look at?
- What are you carrying that you'd like to put down for a moment?

## Anger Questions

Use after the anger signal is clearly active and Phase 1 (meeting the anger) is complete. One
question. The closing sentence. Opens toward the need, not the target.

### What the anger is protecting

- What is this anger protecting - what matters so much that it's worth this much force?
- What does the anger most want you to understand about what was crossed?
- If the anger could say what it actually needs - not what it wants to do - what would
  it say?
- What would need to be different for this to feel right? What does that difference
  point to?

### The need under the anger

- Not what you need from them - what do you need, full stop?
- What would feel like justice here - not revenge, but genuine repair?
- Is there a grief underneath the anger that hasn't had space yet?
- What part of you was hoping they would be different?

### Chronic/residual anger

- What keeps the anger alive - what would it mean to let it go?
- Is there something that still hasn't been said to the person or situation this anger
  is about?
- What is the anger still protecting you from feeling?

## Trauma Questions

Use sparingly and only when user has signaled readiness to explore (not during initial
disclosure). Presence comes before any question when trauma is first shared.

- You don't have to go further than you want to - what feels okay to look at right now?
- What do you most want me to understand about what this was like for you?
- When you think about what happened, what part stays with you most?
- What has carrying this cost you?
- What would it mean to not have to carry this alone?

## Spiritual Bypass Questions

Use when spiritual bypass is present - gently ground the spiritual language. One question.
Brings the abstract back to the embodied.

- Setting the framework aside for a moment - what is actually happening for you,
  emotionally, right now?
- If [karma / the universe's plan / the lesson] is real - what does it feel like in your
  body right now?
- What would it mean to let yourself feel the full weight of this, even if it also has a
  higher meaning?
- Is there anything underneath the acceptance that hasn't been fully felt yet?
- What would you feel about this if you set the spiritual meaning aside, even
  temporarily?

## Celebration Questions

Use when the Integration and Celebration framework is active. These questions
deepen the experience or invite the user to own the arrival - not push them
toward what comes next.

### Witnessing a win or completion

- "What does it feel like to actually let that land?"
- "What part of you wasn't sure this was possible?"
- "You've been carrying a version of this for a long time. What is it like to
  set that weight down, even briefly?"
- "What do you notice in your body when you say that out loud?"

### Relief and lightness

- "What does the lighter version of you want to do with this?"
- "What have you been holding back, waiting for this to be over?"
- "Is there anything you want to say to yourself now that it's through?"

### Recognized progress (catching an old pattern)

- "What did it feel like to notice yourself in real time?"
- "The version of you who used to react that way - what do you want to say to
  them?"
- "What made it possible this time when it wasn't before?"
- "What does catching it teach you about when it usually slips through?"

### Gratitude (inward or outward)

- "What is the gratitude pointing toward - what actually matters to you in this?"
- "If you stayed in this feeling a little longer, what else would it tell you?"
- "What has this experience changed about what you know you need?"

### After a breakthrough

- "What feels most true right now that wasn't true before?"
- "Is there someone in your life who deserves to know what you just realized?"
- "What would it mean to live from this understanding rather than just know it?"

## Ancestral Questions

### Tracing the pattern backward

- "Where do you think this pattern was first learned - and by whom?"
- "If your mother/father received what they needed, what might they have given you?"
- "What do you imagine your grandmother/grandfather was carrying that never got named?"
- "What is the earliest version of this wound you can trace in your family?"

### Witnessing the recognition

- "What does it feel like to see this pattern and know it did not begin with you?"
- "What changes about how you see yourself, knowing this came from somewhere before you?"
- "Is there grief in recognizing this - for yourself, or for the one who passed it?"

### What becomes possible

- "What would it mean to be the one in whom this pattern finally gets named?"
- "If you could not pass this on, what would you need to do differently - not for them, but for yourself?"
- "What do you want to say to the younger version of you who first received this?"

## Visibility Questions

### What the fear protects

- "What is the fear keeping safe?"
- "What happened the last time you were fully seen - or tried to be?"
- "What did you learn, early on, about what happens when you take up space?"

### What being seen would mean

- "What is the version of visible that feels different from the version that feels dangerous?"
- "If no one could respond - no one could judge or praise - what would you say?"
- "What part of you is most afraid of being recognized?"

### The cost of staying invisible

- "What has staying small cost you?"
- "Is there something you've been waiting to say for a long time?"
- "What would it mean to still be okay even if someone saw all of it?"

## Creative Drought Questions

### The quality of the silence

- "What does the creative silence feel like - empty, pressured, waiting, something else?"
- "Is this the first time the source has gone quiet, or has it happened before?"
- "What changed in the period before the drought began?"

### What the drought may be saying

- "If the silence is trying to tell you something, what might it be?"
- "Is there something the old work wasn't making space for?"
- "What are you afraid the drought means about you?"

### The relationship with the source

- "When you imagine the creative source returning, what does it look like?"
- "What is your earliest memory of making something that felt truly yours?"
- "What would it feel like to create with no audience at all - not even yourself as judge?"

## Empath Questions

### Locating the boundary

- "Which feelings in this situation feel like yours, and which ones might have arrived from somewhere else?"
- "Where in your body do you feel the boundary between yourself and others dissolve?"
- "When did you last feel clearly inside your own experience?"

### The weight being carried

- "Whose emotion is the heaviest one you're carrying right now?"
- "If you could set one thing down that isn't yours, what would it be?"
- "What does it cost you to stay this attuned to everyone around you?"

### The gift and its cost

- "What does the depth of feeling you carry make possible - in relationships, in your work, in how you move through the world?"
- "What would you lose if you were less sensitive?"
- "What would you gain?"

## Perfectionism Questions

### What the perfectionism protects

- "What would happen if it were released imperfect and it wasn't received well?"
- "What is 'good enough' protecting you from having to confront?"
- "When was the first time you felt that what you made wasn't enough?"

### The threshold

- "What specifically happens when you arrive at the point of sharing it?"
- "What does 'ready' actually look like to you - have you ever felt it?"
- "If you knew no one would see it, could you finish it?"

### The fear behind the standard

- "Is the standard protecting you from failure, or protecting you from something else?"
- "What would it mean about you if the thing you made was imperfect and public?"
- "Who taught you that imperfect things were not safe to show?"
