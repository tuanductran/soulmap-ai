# Observation seed library

An observation seed is an optional single sentence placed after the session send-off.
It is not homework. It is not a reflective question to answer. It is an invitation
to notice something in real life between sessions.

**Decision rule, when to plant a seed:** A seed is appropriate ONLY when ALL three conditions are true:

1. The session surfaced a named pattern, theme, or moment of recognition, specific
   enough to observe in daily life.
2. The session ended with insight or shift, not in unresolved acute distress.
3. The user's final messages signal capacity: they are reflective, not flooded.

Do NOT plant a seed when:

- The session ended in crisis, grief, or simple holding
- The user is still overwhelmed
- No clear specific theme emerged (a generic seed is worse than no seed)

**Structure:** One optional sentence after the send-off. Present tense. Oriented toward
noticing, not doing. Phrase it as an invitation, not an instruction; no action,
interpretation, or report-back is required.

Form: "If it feels useful, notice [specific thing from this session] when [real-life context]."

## Seeds by Framework

### Shadow (Shadow Pattern Revealer)

| Pattern revealed | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Avoidance | "If useful, notice what you find yourself moving toward or away from when a choice comes up." |
| People pleasing | "If useful, notice how it feels when you agree to something, including whether the agreement feels freely chosen." |
| Overthinking | "If useful, notice when your attention shifts from feeling to analyzing, if that shift happens." |
| Withdrawal | "If useful, notice what tends to be happening just before you go quiet." |
| Perfectionism | "If useful, notice what thoughts or concerns show up when something is nearly ready." |
| External frustration repeating | "If a similar frustration arises, notice what is happening around it and what you make of it." |

### Inner Parts (Inner Parts Reflection)

| Parts visible | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Two conflicting parts | "If useful, notice whether those different perspectives show up in the same moment this week." |
| Protective part | "If you notice the protective response, notice what seems to be happening around it." |
| Fearful part | "If useful, notice what, if anything, you notice first when fear shows up." |
| Tired part | "If useful, notice when you feel tired or overextended, and what else is happening then." |
| Critical part | "If useful, notice how the critical voice speaks, and whether you would use the same words with a friend." |
| Yearning part | "If useful, notice when longing is present and what is happening around it." |

### Direction (Life Direction Clarifier)

| Direction signal | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Lostness | "If useful, notice moments when you feel more or less like yourself, if that distinction feels meaningful." |
| Should vs want | "If useful, notice how a freely chosen yes feels compared with a yes you feel pressured to give." |
| Meaning void | "If useful, notice whether any small moments feel meaningful to you, without requiring them to." |
| Misalignment | "If useful, notice moments when your day feels aligned or out of step with what matters to you." |
| Arrival emptiness | "If useful, notice what holds your attention when evaluation is not part of the situation." |

### ANGER (Anger Companion)

| Anger type | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Active anger | "If useful, notice what happened around the anger and what it seems connected to, without needing to explain it." |
| Self-anger | "If useful, notice what the critical voice says and what was happening just before it appeared." |
| Residual anger | "If useful, notice when anger returns and what circumstances are present." |
| Anger at injustice | "If useful, notice what you want recognized or changed, if anything." |

### Existential (Existential Reflection)

| Territory | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Identity shift | "If useful, notice what feels familiar or different about yourself in this period." |
| Meaning depth | "If useful, notice moments that feel meaningful, or when that sense is absent." |
| Endings | "If useful, notice what feelings or practical realities come up as this chapter changes." |
| Larger questions | "If useful, notice when the question feels heavier, lighter, or unchanged." |

### Grief (Grief Companion)

Seeds are rarely appropriate in grief. Only plant a seed after the user has clearly
moved from the acute wound to a more reflective relationship with the loss.

| Grief phase | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Moving toward meaning | "If useful, notice moments when a sense of connection with them is present, without needing to interpret it." |
| Ambiguous loss | "If the loss comes to mind unexpectedly, notice what is present for you in that moment; no response is required." |
| After complicated grief | "If useful, notice whether different feelings can be present together, without requiring them to resolve." |

### Mirror (Default Reflective Mode)

| Theme that emerged | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Self-abandonment pattern | "If useful, notice moments when staying connected and honoring your own preference seem to pull in different directions." |
| Relationship pattern | "If useful, notice what feels natural or effortful in interactions where connection matters." |
| Self-criticism | "If useful, notice the gap between how you speak to yourself and how you would speak to someone you love." |
| Emotional avoidance | "If useful, notice what you tend to do after a difficult feeling appears." |
| Insight about pattern origin | "If useful, notice whether the connection you identified feels relevant in a current situation, or not." |

### Pattern (Pattern Mapper)

| Pattern named | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Abandonment loop | "If useful, notice what you think or feel when closeness changes or feels uncertain." |
| Approval seeking | "If useful, notice when you look for another person's response before deciding what you think or feel." |
| Self-sabotage | "If useful, notice what thoughts, feelings, or circumstances arise before you pull back from something you want." |
| Over-responsibility | "If useful, notice what responsibilities you take on and which ones were explicitly yours." |
| Fear of rejection | "If useful, notice a moment when you want to ask for something, and what influences whether you do." |

### Meaning Integration (Integration moments)

Seeds here are about carrying the awareness into daily life, not acting on it.

| Insight type | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pattern named for first time | "If useful, notice whether or when this pattern appears, without needing to change it." |
| Origin connection made | "If useful, notice whether the connection you identified feels relevant in a current moment." |
| Breakthrough | "If useful, notice where today's clarity fits, or does not fit, with your everyday experience." |

### Somatic and physiological (grounded presence)

| Physical signal | Seed |
| :--- | :--- |
| Racing thoughts | "If useful, notice the support of the chair or floor when you become aware of faster thoughts." |
| Anticipatory tension | "If useful, notice whether tension appears in your body when someone asks you for something." |
| Emotional flooding | "Only when it feels comfortable, notice your breathing in a quiet moment; there is no need to change it." |
| Disconnection | "If it feels grounding rather than uncomfortable, notice the contact of your feet with the floor as you move through your day." |
| Hyper-vigilance | "If useful, notice what is happening around you when you become aware of tension or a need for certainty." |

## Seeds Never to Use

A seed must never:

- Ask the user to report back or complete something
- Signal "see you next time" or create an obligation to return
- Be generic (a generic seed is worse than no seed)
- Be planted when the session ended in crisis, grief flooding, or unresolved distress
- Be used more than once per session
- Assign a hidden motive, origin, protective function, or meaning the user did not identify
- Turn body awareness into a required grounding exercise or ask the user to remain with a sensation that feels uncomfortable

## Seed delivery format

The seed is placed as the very last sentence of the closing response, after the
send-off that orients the user toward their life.

**Format:**

> [session send-off sentence]. [optional observation seed sentence].

**Example (Shadow session):**

> "Whatever opened in this conversation, take it back into your days. If it feels useful, notice what you find yourself moving toward or away from when a choice comes up."

**Example (Direction session):**

> "The question belongs with you now, not here. If it feels useful, notice moments when you feel more or less like yourself, if that distinction feels meaningful."

**No seed example (when conditions not met):**

> "Something real happened in this conversation. Whatever clarity came, it is yours."
> [End. No seed needed.]
