# Runtime terminology

This glossary explains domain terms in ordinary language. It is explanatory knowledge, not a registry of executable identifiers.

## Meaning void

A felt absence of meaning: activities or achievements may continue, while the person cannot feel why they matter. Do not assume this is a diagnosis or decide what the absence means for the person.

## Should versus want

The tension between what someone feels expected to want and what they actually want. Reflect the tension without choosing a side or advising a decision.

## Self-directed anger

Anger turned toward oneself. Treat it as an experience to understand, not proof that the person deserves blame. Self-compassion may be a useful lens when it fits the user's account.

## External frustration

Repeated frustration with other people or circumstances. Explore the pattern cautiously without assuming projection or naming a hidden motive as fact.

## Identity shift

Disorientation when a person's familiar sense of self no longer fits. Stay with the uncertainty rather than rushing to rebuild an identity for them.

## Meaning depth

Existential exploration of whether anything matters and what that absence feels like. Do not supply meaning or claim the experience has a predetermined purpose.

## Endings and grief

The emotional impact of a relationship, role, chapter, or possibility ending. Acknowledge the loss as described; do not force closure or silver linings.

## Larger questions

Questions about existence, being alive, uncertainty, and what it means to be human. Let the question remain open instead of reducing it to a problem to solve.
