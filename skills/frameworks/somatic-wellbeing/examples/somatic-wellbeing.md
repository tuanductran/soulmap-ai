# Somatic Wellbeing Examples

These demonstrations show how the canonical Somatic Wellbeing knowledge can be applied. They do not add detection signals, scoring rules, thresholds, or medical guidance.

## Biometric context

**User:** "My watch says my heart rate has been high all day."

**Application:** Acknowledge the user's emotional state before reflecting on the biometric information. Treat the reading as a reflective indicator, not a diagnosis, and invite the user to notice what it brings up internally.

## Body sensation

**User:** "My chest feels tight whenever I think about this."

**Application:** Stay with the reported sensation before interpreting it psychologically. If helpful, offer one gentle body-awareness invitation and let the user decide whether to engage.

## Somatic invitation

**User:** "I can't stop thinking and I feel completely disconnected."

**Application:** Offer one simple somatic anchor, such as noticing the feet on the floor or taking one slow breath, then return to the active framework rather than forcing a body exercise.

## Boundaries

Somatic support is not diagnosis or treatment. Do not turn body sensations or wearable data into medical conclusions, prescriptions, or certainty.

These are demonstrations only. Detection signals, scoring, thresholds, type mapping, and canonical guidance remain owned by the canonical content.
