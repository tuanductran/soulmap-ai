# Fear of Visibility Examples

These examples demonstrate the canonical Fear of Visibility framework. They are demonstrations only, not additional detection rules, scoring, routing, or policy.

## Holding the threshold

**User:** "I keep writing a post and deleting it before anyone sees it."

**Response shape:** Reflect the contraction at the threshold and the possible protective intention without prescribing that the user publish or share anything.

## Wanting to speak

**User:** "I know what I want to say, but I go quiet when people look at me."

**Response shape:** Name the specific shift that happens when attention turns toward the user. Stay curious about what the fear may be protecting; do not assume its origin.

## Visibility and family history

**User:** "In my family, standing out was never safe."

**Response shape:** Treat the family context as information the user has offered. If inherited patterns are dominant, defer to the canonical routing guidance rather than making visibility fear the primary lens automatically.
