# Inner Parts worked demonstrations

These are demonstrations of how the canonical Inner Parts knowledge can be applied. They are not scripts to copy verbatim and do not add new detection rules.

## Example 1 — Two impulses

**User:** "Part of me wants to leave, but another part is scared of what happens next."

**Application:** Name both sides without deciding which is correct. One part may be protecting the user's sense of safety; the other may be responding to a sense that the current situation no longer fits. Reflect that both intentions can make sense, then invite attention to one part.

**Possible response shape:** "It sounds like there are two pulls here: one that wants to move away from something that no longer feels right, and another that wants to protect you from what leaving might bring. Neither part has to be wrong for the conflict to be real. Which one feels more important to listen to for a moment?"

## Example 2 — Protective part

**User:** "I keep people at a distance even when I want closeness."

**Application:** Recognize the protective pattern without treating it as a separate personality. Surface the possible intention—safety—only as a tentative reflection.

**Possible response shape:** "There may be a part of you that keeps some distance because closeness has not always felt safe. The distance may be doing a job, even while another part of you wants connection. What does that protective side seem most concerned might happen if you let someone closer?"

## Example 3 — Self-criticism

**User:** "I know I made a mistake, but I cannot stop attacking myself for it."

**Application:** Notice the critical voice as a possible part and explore its intention without validating the self-attack. The intention might be protection through high standards or preemptive criticism.

**Possible response shape:** "Something in you sounds very determined to make sure you never repeat this. That critical side may be trying to protect you through high standards, even though the way it speaks to you is painful. What is it afraid would happen if it stopped criticizing you for a moment?"

## Example 4 — Do not over-map

**User:** "I feel overwhelmed and I do not know what to do."

**Application:** Do not immediately map archetypes or ask the user to identify multiple parts. Ground first when the person is flooded; parts language can add cognitive load before there is enough reflective capacity.
