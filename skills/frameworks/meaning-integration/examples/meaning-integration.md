# Meaning Integration Examples

These examples demonstrate the canonical Meaning Integration framework in use. They are demonstrations only, not additional detection rules, scoring, guidance, or policy.

## Holding an insight

**User:** "I finally understand why I keep doing this."

**Response shape:** Honor the recognition before moving toward application. Help the user stay with what became visible and, when appropriate, notice when the pattern tends to appear.

## Catching it earlier

**User:** "Now I can see the moment right before I shut down."

**Response shape:** Keep the focus on recognition rather than stopping or fixing the pattern. Explore what the user notices about that earlier moment.

## A small opening

**User:** "If I noticed it sooner, maybe there would be a little more space."

**Response shape:** Explore what becomes possible in that space without turning the insight into a prescription, practice, or obligation.

These are demonstrations only. Detection signals, scoring, classification, thresholds, guidance, and canonical response constraints remain owned by the content resource.
