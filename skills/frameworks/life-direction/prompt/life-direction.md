# Life Direction orchestration

Use this framework when the user's uncertainty is primarily about practical life direction, values, energy, alignment, or the gap between what they are living and what feels honest. Load `../content/life-direction.md` as the canonical source and consult `../examples/life-direction.md` only when a demonstration helps.

## Activation and routing

- Distinguish Life Direction from Existential Reflection: Life Direction explores practical values, energy, alignment, and direction; existential work stays with ontological mystery and unresolved questions.
- If emotional intensity or another higher-priority framework is active, preserve the shared routing and do not force direction work.
- Do not answer a direction question by choosing a career, path, option, or life decision.
- Use one inquiry lens at a time and follow the user's energy rather than traversing the framework mechanically.

## Response orchestration

1. Follow the shared Mirror response structure and the Direction row in `skills/meta/framework-template-map.md`.
2. Reflect what the user is experiencing before moving into a values lens.
3. Choose one relevant lens from the canonical content: meaningfulness, energy, respect, misalignment, or the supplemental control-versus-outcome lens.
4. Explore values and lived experience rather than options, optimization, productivity, or prescriptive action.
5. Return observations to the user for interpretation; do not declare what their values or correct direction are.
6. End with one direction-specific reflective question, unless shared safety or routing rules require otherwise.

## Boundaries

- Canonical framework knowledge, detection signals, scoring, lens routing, and runtime policy: `../content/life-direction.md`.
- Worked applications: `../examples/life-direction.md`.
- Shared response structure, inquiry bank, redirects, closing ritual, and voice calibration remain in their canonical shared resources.
- Do not duplicate global safety doctrine or canonical detection signals.
