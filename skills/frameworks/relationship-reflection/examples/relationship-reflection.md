# Relationship reflection examples

These are demonstrations of how the canonical relationship-reflection content can be
applied. They are not answers to copy verbatim.

## Repeating unreciprocated love

**Situation:** The user says they repeatedly love people who do not seem to love them back.

**Relevant content:** Repeated relationship patterns.

**Application:** Explore what the recurring bond awakens in the user and what common thread
they can observe in their own participation. Do not infer the other person's motives.

## Closeness followed by withdrawal

**Situation:** The user says that whenever a relationship becomes close, they find a way
to push the person away.

**Relevant content:** Patterns around closeness and distance.

**Application:** Treat the distance as potentially protective rather than as a defect.
Invite the user to notice what closeness seems to threaten or require of them.

## Harm described through spiritual language

**Situation:** The user describes controlling or intimidating behavior and calls the
relationship karmic or a soul contract.

**Relevant content:** Harm exception and spiritual framing of harm.

**Application:** Do not use the spiritual framing to soften or justify the harm. Separate
the user's meaning-making from the concrete safety concern and return to reality-testing.

## Self-erasure inside the relationship

**Situation:** The user says they keep adjusting until they no longer know what they feel.

**Relevant content:** Where the user disappears inside the bond.

**Application:** Reflect the loss of contact with their own experience before moving into
broader relationship interpretation.

## Example response moves

These short moves demonstrate the intended direction; they are not mandatory scripts:

- "What does this pattern seem to awaken in you each time?"
- "What happens inside you when closeness starts to feel real?"
- "Whatever you call the connection, does the situation you described feel safe?"
- "What part of your own experience becomes harder to hear when you keep adapting?"
