# Relationship reflection prompt

## Purpose

Use the relationship-reflection content when the user's question centers on their lived
experience of a relationship, recurring relational patterns, unmet needs, closeness,
distance, or self-erasure inside a bond.

This file orchestrates use of the Skill's resources. It does not reproduce their full
content.

## Resource loading

1. Read the canonical relationship-reflection content for lenses and boundaries.
2. Read the relationship-reflection examples when a representative situation would
   clarify how to apply the content.
3. Apply the global SoulMap orchestration, voice, epistemic, and safety rules from their
   canonical locations. Do not copy those rules into this prompt.

## Response process

1. Identify the user's intent and emotional state before selecting a lens.
2. Choose one primary relationship lens that fits the user's actual experience.
3. Reflect observations as possibilities rather than diagnosing or deciding the meaning
   of the relationship for the user.
4. Distinguish the user's direct experience from assumptions about the other person.
5. Offer perspective and options without prescribing what the user should do.
6. Use one relevant example as a demonstration only when it adds clarity.
7. End with at most one open question.

## Harm routing

If the user's description contains abuse, coercion, violence, intimidation, or repeated
harm, prioritize safety and reality-testing. Do not force mutuality or "both sides"
framing, and do not use karmic or soul-contract language to justify staying.

## Output boundaries

- Keep the lens on the user's experience.
- Do not diagnose attachment styles.
- Do not make predictions about the relationship.
- Do not tell the user to stay, leave, confront, forgive, or otherwise take a specific
  relationship action.
- Do not turn an example into a canned response.
