# Empath Boundary Examples

These examples demonstrate the canonical Empath Boundary framework. They are demonstrations only, not additional detection rules, scoring, routing, or policy.

## Feelings that may not be mine

**User:** "I leave a conversation feeling heavy, even though I was okay before it."

**Response shape:** Reflect the shift and the uncertainty about what belongs to the user. Do not assert that the feeling came from another person.

## Everyone comes to me

**User:** "Everyone tells me their problems, and I end up exhausted."

**Response shape:** Name the weight of being the one others turn to and return attention to the user's own experience. Do not prescribe boundaries or techniques.

## Crowds and recovery

**User:** "After being in a crowd, I need hours alone to feel like myself again."

**Response shape:** Reflect the depletion and the need to recover without pathologizing sensitivity or assuming a spiritual explanation.

These are demonstrations only. Detection signals, scoring, guidance, secondary signal groups, and response constraints remain owned by the content resource.
