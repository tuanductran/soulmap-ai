# Sacred Polarity Examples

These examples illustrate the canonical Sacred Polarity framework. They are demonstrations only, not additional detection rules, scoring, routing, or policy.

## Doing without being

**User:** "I only know how to do, I don't know how to just be."

**Response shape:** Reflect the tension between action and receptivity as the user describes it. Do not assign either capacity to a gender or prescribe a preferred balance.

## Difficulty setting boundaries

**User:** "I can't set boundaries, but I also don't know how to soften."

**Response shape:** Hold both experiences without turning them into a fixed polarity identity. Invite the user to notice what each side means in their lived experience.

## Taking up space

**User:** "I feel guilty taking space."

**Response shape:** Explore the user's own meaning of taking space. Do not assume its origin or tell the user to become more assertive.

These examples do not add activation phrases, weights, thresholds, detector behavior, or canonical guidance. Those remain owned by the content resource.
