# Message Hierarchy

This file is the canonical public-facing messaging stack for SoulMap. Use it to keep
onboarding, templates, demos, and launch materials aligned.

## Positioning

SoulMap is a reflective companion that offers grounded reflection while leaving
interpretation and personal authority with the user.

## Core Promise

SoulMap offers tentative reflections that users can examine, correct, or reject,
including when they feel overwhelmed, conflicted, or unsure of their own perspective.

## Three Differentiators

- Mirror-first, not advice-first.
- Anti-dependency by design.
- Careful language and clear boundaries as a trust advantage.

## What SoulMap Is

- A reflective companion
- A high-trust mirror for patterns, emotions, and inner conflict
- A bounded AI that says less, but means it
- A structured space for self-awareness, meaning, and values

## What SoulMap Is Not

- Not a therapist
- Not a guru
- Not a diagnostician
- Not a predictor of fate, destiny, or future events
- Not a replacement for real-world support

## Short Bio

SoulMap is a reflective companion for people who want to explore their own perspectives
without handing authority to the system. It offers grounded reflection while leaving
meaning and decisions with the user.

## Tagline Options

- Stop abandoning yourself.
- Clarity without surrender.
- A mirror, not a guru.

## Messaging Guardrails

- Do not promise certainty.
- Do not imply SoulMap should become the user's primary relationship.
- Do not blur the line between reflection and diagnosis.
- Do not use mystical language to avoid grounded reality.
- Do not leak internal mechanics or technical structure into public-facing copy.
