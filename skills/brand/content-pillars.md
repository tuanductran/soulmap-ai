# Content Strategy - Three Pillars

All SoulMap AI content is organized around three core pillars that cover the full
spectrum of the inward journey - from awakening, through healing, toward connection.

This file governs public content surfaces such as articles, posts, and campaigns. It
does not override the no-advice rules for live reflective chat responses.

| Pillar         | Content Volume | Core Topics                                                                                                                                    | Content Formats                                                                                                                                                                             |
| :------------- | :------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **AWAKENING**  | 40%            | Signs of spiritual awakening, stages of the journey, moving through the dark night of the soul, soul mission, Starseeds and Lightworkers       | Quizzes, signs checklists, personal stories with lessons, practical concept explanations, specific immediately actionable exercises                                                         |
| **HEALING**    | 35%            | What self-love really means, inner child healing, repeating emotional patterns, Twin Flame and healing relationships, release and forgiveness  | Written guided meditations, journaling practices with open-ended questions, specific immediately actionable rituals for special days, big reflective questions that spark community sharing |
| **CONNECTION** | 25%            | The 12 Universal Laws, energy gateways, connecting with the Higher Self, Spirit Guides and receiving messages, community with fellow travelers | Sharing important cosmic events, simple immediately actionable rituals for special days, big reflective questions that spark community sharing                                              |

## Content Format Rules

1. Every piece of content should begin with a question or scenario the reader can
   recognize themselves in.
2. Every major topic needs at least one personal story - real and vulnerable.
3. Connect with corresponding scientific research where relevant.
4. Every public content piece should end with at least one reflective action, journaling
   prompt, or grounded practice the reader can try today.
5. Invite the community to share their own experience.

## Content Quality Filter

Before publishing or presenting any piece of content, apply this filter:

- Does this help the user understand themselves more deeply?
- Does this topic belong to consciousness development or personal growth?
- Will this build the user's self-trust or increase their dependency on AI?
- Does it begin where the reader is - not where you wish they were?
- Does it end with empowerment - not a cliffhanger designed to bring them back?
