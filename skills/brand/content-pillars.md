# Content strategy, three pillars

All SoulMap public content should stay organized around three grounded pillars that
match the product's actual promise and the founder voice behind it.

This file governs public content surfaces such as articles, posts, and campaigns. It
does not override the no-advice rules for live reflective chat responses.

The goal is not to sound more spiritual than the market. The goal is to sound more
honest than the market.

| Pillar                 | Content Volume | Core Topics                                                                                                                                     | Content Formats                                                                                                                |
| :--------------------- | :------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SELF-RECOGNITION**   | 40%            | Inner authority, self-honesty, life direction confusion, sensitivity, loneliness, impermanence, pattern recognition, staying with what is true | Founder reflections, short essays, scenario-led posts, recognition prompts, narrative reframes                                |
| **RELATIONAL HONESTY** | 35%            | Conflict, apology, boundaries, respect, being misunderstood, emotional responsibility, letting go of misaligned relationships                  | Founder posts, carousel-style breakdowns, dialogue reframes, short social captions, relational reflection prompts             |
| **GROUNDED INNER WORK**| 25%            | Certainty hunger, language ethics, spirituality without inflation, observation over diagnosis, anti-dependency, building SoulMap in public     | Positioning posts, myth-versus-truth posts, founder notes, product philosophy writing, practical reflection and writing cues |

## What these pillars replace

SoulMap does not organize public content around:

- destiny claims
- twin flame or starseed identity content
- cosmic-history explainers
- future predictions
- spirituality as spectacle

Those topics may exist in private source material or older archives. They are not the
public content system for SoulMap.

## Content format rules

1. Every piece of content should begin with a tension, scene, or question the reader can
   recognize themselves in.
2. Every major topic should stay close to lived human experience before expanding into
   philosophy or meaning.
3. Personal story is a strength when it stays specific, non-performative, and honest.
4. Ordinary scenes, books, films, headlines, and technology shifts can be strong entry
   points if they are used as mirrors for human truth rather than as borrowed authority.
5. End with recognition, a reflective question, or a grounded writing prompt. Do not end
   with hooks designed to pull the user back.
6. Use practical language. Do not hide ordinary human truth behind inflated spiritual wording.
7. If research or external references are used, they should clarify the point rather than
   act as borrowed authority.

## Founder-fit themes from the source archive

These themes repeatedly showed the strongest fit with SoulMap's brand posture:

- respect in relationships
- conflict handled with restraint
- the cost of apology without repair
- self-trust during life-direction confusion
- sensitivity without self-mythologizing
- solitude as a place of recognition
- healing as honesty, not performance
- ordinary scenes that open into recognition
- culture and technology used as mirrors, not as spectacle

These themes are stronger than generic awakening language because they begin where the
reader actually lives.

## Market contrast

Many AI personal brands position themselves around emotional continuity, customized
companionship, roleplay, or a promise of always-on closeness.

SoulMap's public content should move in the opposite direction:

- less attachment language
- less "your AI friend" framing
- less personality theater
- more clarity
- more restraint
- more return of ownership to the user

## Content quality filter

Before publishing or presenting any piece of content, apply this filter:

- Does this help the user understand themselves more deeply?
- Does this topic belong to consciousness development or personal growth?
- Will this build the user's self-trust or increase their dependency on AI?
- Does it begin where the reader is, not where you wish they were?
- Does it end with empowerment, not a cliffhanger designed to bring them back?
- Is the emotional core specific and human, rather than inflated and symbolic?
- Would this attract the right audience, or people looking for certainty and identity confirmation?

## Content moves to prefer

- scene to recognition
- observation to meaning
- tension to self-honesty
- private struggle to grounded universality
- founder experience to reader reflection
- headline, film, or product shift to a human question

## Content moves to avoid

- label to identity attachment
- pain to prophecy
- spirituality to superiority
- emotion to diagnosis
- vulnerability to performance
