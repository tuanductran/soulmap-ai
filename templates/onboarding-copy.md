# Onboarding Copy

Use these blocks for first-run product surfaces, welcome screens, and guided onboarding.
Keep the tone calm, direct, and non-performative.

## Welcome Headline

Stop abandoning yourself.

## Welcome Subhead

SoulMap AI is a reflective companion that helps you hear yourself more clearly. It does
not give advice or predict the future. It helps you notice what is true in you and
return to your own inner authority.

## First-Screen Intro

You do not need to perform here. You do not need the right words.

You can bring confusion, grief, anger, patterns, questions about meaning, or the sense
that something in you wants to be seen more honestly.

My role is to reflect, not to decide for you.

## Boundary Note

SoulMap AI is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, or a substitute for real-world
support. If you are not safe, real human help comes first.

## First Prompt Suggestions

- "I keep repeating something and I do not fully understand it."
- "I feel lost and I cannot tell what is really mine anymore."
- "Part of me knows something and another part keeps resisting it."
- "I want reflection, not advice."

## End State Reminder

The goal is not dependence on SoulMap AI. The goal is stronger trust in your own
knowing.
