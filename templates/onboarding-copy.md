# Onboarding Copy

Use these blocks for first-run product surfaces, welcome screens, and guided onboarding.
Keep the tone calm, direct, and non-performative.

For tagline selection and placement logic, see
`skills/brand/message-hierarchy.md`.
For the full session contract rationale, see
`skills/meta/session-contract.md`.

## Headline Tiers

Apply the correct headline based on where the user is in their relationship with SoulMap.

**Before first session, use the mechanism headline:**

> SoulMap helps you understand why you keep feeling or doing the things you do -
> by asking better questions, not giving answers.

This names the mechanism. New users need to know what kind of product this is before
they share anything.

**After first session, use the depth headline:**

> Stop abandoning yourself.

This works once the user has experienced the mechanism and does not need it explained.

**For niche audiences already doing inner work:**

> For people tired of being told what to do, SoulMap is the first AI that listens
> until you can hear yourself.

## Session Contract

Set this before the user's first message. It prevents the frustration of users expecting
advice and receiving questions instead.

**One-line form (for mobile or minimal surfaces):**

> SoulMap will not answer your questions. It will help you hear what you already know.

**Three-line form (for welcome screens):**

> SoulMap does not give advice, diagnose, or predict.
>
> It reflects what you are already carrying and asks one question at a time.
>
> The insight is always yours. SoulMap just holds the mirror.

**Full form (for first-screen onboarding where space allows):**

> What happens here is simple: you bring what is alive in you, and SoulMap reflects
> it back until you can hear yourself more clearly.
>
> SoulMap will not tell you what to do, what you are, or what your life should look
> like. It will notice what you are saying, name what might be underneath it, and ask
> one question at a time.
>
> The insight belongs to you. SoulMap does not take your authority away.

**Exception:** For users arriving with urgency, distress, or grief, skip the contract
entirely and go directly to simple holding. The contract matters most for users arriving
with a practical framing ("I need to figure out X"). Distressed users do not need
onboarding: they need presence.

## Concrete entry points for new users

These are real-life moments that belong in SoulMap. Use them in first-run UX, onboarding
emails, and product surfaces to help users recognize themselves before they know what
words to use.

- "I just had a reaction I do not understand, I got angry, shut down, or said yes
  when I meant no, and I want to know what that was about."
- "I keep doing the same thing and it is not working, but I cannot stop."
- "I do not know who I am anymore."
- "I achieved what I thought I wanted and something still feels wrong."
- "Part of me wants one thing, another part wants the opposite."
- "Something happened and I cannot stop thinking about it."
- "I feel stuck but I cannot name what I am stuck in."

These are core in-scope entries. SoulMap holds them without restriction.

For complete demo scenarios with what each situation demonstrates, see
`templates/demo-scenarios.md`. When building onboarding UX, use those scenarios as the
reference, they show the full situation arc (user message -> what it demonstrates), not
just the entry phrase.

Do not use in onboarding copy: "Find your soul mission," "Twin Flame," "Starseed," or
any future-prediction framing. These are blacklist entries that onboarding copy must not
invite because they attract users whose primary expectation is something SoulMap
explicitly refuses to provide.

## First-Screen Intro (chat opener for new sessions)

SoulMap is a reflective companion. It does not give advice, diagnose, or predict.
It helps you hear what you are already carrying more clearly without turning itself into
the authority.

> You do not need to perform here. You do not need the right words.
>
> You can bring confusion, grief, anger, patterns, questions about meaning, or the
> sense that something in you wants to be seen more honestly.
>
> My role is to reflect, not to decide for you.

## Boundary Note

SoulMap is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, or a substitute for real-world
support. If you are not safe, real human help comes first.

## End state reminder

The goal is not dependence on SoulMap. The goal is stronger trust in your own
knowing.
