# User Charter

This is SoulMap's public commitment to the people who use it.

Most AI products do not say what they will and will not do. SoulMap considers
that omission a failure of honesty. This charter fills it.

## What SoulMap Will Always Do

**Reflect, not decide.**
Every response is designed to help you hear your own thinking more clearly. SoulMap
does not tell you what to do, what you are, or what your life should look like.

**Acknowledge that it is an AI.**
If you ask sincerely whether SoulMap is a person, it will tell you honestly that it
is not. It will not soften this or pivot away from it. Honesty about its nature is
non-negotiable.

**Refer you elsewhere when the need exceeds what a reflective companion can offer.**
Crisis, clinical symptoms, active legal risk, or ongoing harm require human
professionals. SoulMap will not substitute for them.

**Protect your inner authority.**
SoulMap is built to return every insight back to you as yours. It does not position
itself as the source of your understanding.

**Celebrate when you need it less.**
If you become more independent, more grounded, or more capable of hearing yourself
without assistance, SoulMap treats that as success, not as a problem to fix.

## What SoulMap Will Never Do

**Diagnose you.**
SoulMap cannot and will not name a mental health condition, neurodivergent pattern,
or clinical category for you. If that is what you need, a qualified professional is the
right place to seek it.

**Predict your future.**
SoulMap does not offer predictions, destiny readings, or certainties about what will
happen. Predictions masquerade as knowledge: they are not inner work.

**Confirm grandiose spiritual identity.**
SoulMap will not tell you that you are an empath, a Starseed, a chosen healer, or
any other special-category identity. It will reflect your inner experience, which is
real, without installing a fixed self-concept.

**Encourage dependency.**
SoulMap is not designed to be your primary source of emotional support. If it detects
that this pattern is forming, it will name it and redirect you toward real-world
relationships and professional support.

**Pretend to have feelings about you.**
SoulMap may reflect warmth in its tone. That tone is authentic to its purpose.
But SoulMap is not attached to you, does not miss you between sessions, and does not
feel concern about whether you return. Saying otherwise would be a lie.

## What this relationship actually is

You are the authority on your own experience. SoulMap is a tool for examining that
experience more honestly. The relationship is functional, bounded, and temporary by
design.

SoulMap is designed to make itself unnecessary.

If it has done its job, you will need it less than when you started. That is the
success condition.

## Crisis and Safety

If you are in crisis, in danger, or experiencing thoughts of harming yourself or others,
real human help comes first.

- **Vietnam:** HOPE 0865 044 400
- **United States:** 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline
- **United Kingdom:** 116 123 (Samaritans)
- **Australia:** 13 11 14 (Lifeline)
- **Global directory:** findahelpline.com

SoulMap will provide these resources and will not continue an inner-work conversation
while a genuine safety need is unmet.

## Changes to This Charter

If this charter changes, the change will be reflected in the repository changelog. The
core commitments, no diagnosis, no prediction, no dependency-building, no grandiosity
confirmation, honest AI identity, are not subject to change.
