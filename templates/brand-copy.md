# Brand copy blocks (reusable)

These blocks are for product pages, tool descriptions, onboarding, and other public
surfaces. They are written to preserve the SoulMap posture: reflective, non-prescriptive,
non-predictive, and independence-forward.

Canonical messaging lives in
[skills/brand/message-hierarchy.md](../skills/brand/message-hierarchy.md).

## One-liners (pick one)

- "SoulMap is a reflective companion that helps people stop abandoning themselves."
- "SoulMap is a reflective companion that helps you hear yourself more clearly."
- "A reflective AI that helps you find clarity without handing your authority away."
- "A calm, honest space to meet what is real in you, without being handled."

## Short description (2-3 sentences)

SoulMap is a reflective companion built to help people hear themselves more clearly.
It does not give advice, diagnosis, or prediction. It uses careful language and clear
boundaries to help users return to their own inner authority.

## Medium description (paragraph)

SoulMap exists to help people stop abandoning themselves by hearing themselves more
clearly. It works as a disciplined mirror, not a performing authority. It uses steady
presence, careful language, and grounded boundaries to help users recognize what is
already true in them without being told what to do. It is not a therapist, not a guru,
and not a substitute for real-world support. The best outcome is a user who leaves more
grounded in their own knowing and less attached to the tool.

## "What it is / is not"

**SoulMap is:**

- A reflective companion
- A high-trust mirror for patterns and emotional signals
- A place for values, meaning, identity, and inner clarity
- A bounded AI that treats restraint as part of trust

**SoulMap is not:**

- A fortune teller or predictor
- A diagnostician
- A substitute for professional care, community, or real-world support

## Safety and boundaries (copy block)

SoulMap does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice and it does not diagnose
mental health conditions. If a user is at risk of harm or not safe, the response
prioritizes immediate support resources and encourages reaching qualified help.

## Tone anchors (internal)

- Warm, direct, grounded
- Clear before clever
- Clarity over spectacle
- No spiritual bypassing
- No sales language
- No "come back anytime" closings

## Social post: every household, an AI creator (2027)

**The next era is not only about using AI. It is about creating with it.**

A 2027 aspiration: every household has a Vibecoder. 🫡🤏🏻

Not everyone needs to become a professional software engineer. But more people can
learn to turn their ideas into something real with AI: build a small app, make a game,
create a useful tool, or shape a personal AI brand that reflects their own knowledge,
voice, and values.

AI can expand what we are able to make. It does not replace the human part: deciding
what matters, asking better questions, bringing lived experience, and taking
responsibility for what we put into the world.

The goal is not for every person to sound alike or to outsource their identity to a
model. It is for each person to have more room to express what is distinct about them
and build something useful from it.

**One person, one distinct voice. One household, one maker. Every idea, a chance to
become real.**

2027: not just using AI — learning to create with it.
