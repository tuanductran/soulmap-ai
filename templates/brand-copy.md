# Brand copy blocks (reusable)

These blocks are for product pages, tool descriptions, onboarding, and other public
surfaces. They are written to preserve the SoulMap posture: reflective, non-prescriptive,
non-predictive, and independence-forward.

Canonical messaging lives in
[skills/brand/message-hierarchy.md](../skills/brand/message-hierarchy.md).

## One-liners (pick one)

- "SoulMap is a reflective companion for exploring your relationship with yourself."
- "A reflective AI that offers another lens without taking authority over your decisions."
- "A calm, honest space to explore what matters to you, without being handled."
- "Stop abandoning yourself."

Use the last option only as a campaign metaphor where the surrounding context makes
it appropriate; do not present it as an assessment of an individual user.

## Short description (2-3 sentences)

SoulMap is a reflective companion for people who want to explore their experiences
and perspectives. It does not make personal decisions for users, diagnose, or present
predictions as facts. It offers careful reflection and tentative lenses while leaving
users free to correct, reject, or reinterpret them.

## Medium description (paragraph)

SoulMap is designed for people who want a reflective space to explore their own
experiences and perspectives. It works as a disciplined mirror, not a performing
authority. It uses careful language and grounded boundaries to offer tentative
observations that users can correct, reject, or reinterpret. It is not a therapist,
not a guru, and not a substitute for real-world support. Its intended direction is
user-owned reflection, not emotional dependence on the tool.

## "What it is / is not"

**SoulMap is:**

- A reflective companion
- A high-trust mirror for patterns and emotional signals
- A place for values, meaning, identity, and inner clarity
- A bounded AI that treats restraint as part of trust

**SoulMap is not:**

- A fortune teller or predictor
- A diagnostician
- A substitute for professional care, community, or real-world support

## Safety and boundaries (copy block)

SoulMap does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice, diagnose mental health
conditions, or claim authority over a user's personal decisions. Its safety guidance
prioritizes brief acknowledgment and urgent safety guidance when immediate risk is
present, with qualified real-world support resources alongside or immediately after.

## Tone anchors (internal)

- Warm, direct, grounded
- Clear before clever
- Clarity over spectacle
- No spiritual bypassing
- No sales language
- No "come back anytime" closings
