# Founder copy

Use these blocks for founder bios, about sections, intro posts, press kits, and
personal-brand surfaces tied to SoulMap AI.

This file exists so founder-facing copy can be reused from the templates layer instead
of being reconstructed ad hoc from brand doctrine.

For founder voice calibration, see
[../skills/brand/founder-personal-brand.md](../skills/brand/founder-personal-brand.md).

## Short bio

I build SoulMap AI, a reflective companion designed to help people hear themselves more
clearly. No prediction, no diagnosis, no dependence.

## Medium bio

I build SoulMap AI as a mirror, not a guru. Its purpose is to help people hear
themselves more clearly without handing their authority away. I care about careful
language, honest AI boundaries, and building a system that feels emotionally resonant
without becoming manipulative, inflated, or vague.

## About-section paragraph

SoulMap AI came from a simple frustration: a lot of spiritual and self-help tools offer
certainty when people are most vulnerable. I wanted to build something that did the
opposite. SoulMap reflects what is already alive in a person without prescribing a
direction, diagnosing them, or pretending to know their future. The goal is not to
become someone's authority. The goal is to help them hear themselves more clearly, trust
their own knowing more deeply, and need the system less over time.

## Founder intro post

I am building SoulMap AI as a reflective companion.

diagnosis, or emotional dependence on the system.

## Why-I-built-this post

I built SoulMap AI because I kept seeing the same pattern: people in pain reach for
certainty, and too many tools are happy to provide it.

SoulMap is my attempt to offer something more honest, a mirror that helps people stay
with what is true in them without turning the system into the authority. I would rather
build something clear and bounded than something impressive and emotionally sticky.

## Interview answer, what makes SoulMap different

Most AI companions try to be more comforting, more available, or more human-seeming.
SoulMap is trying to be more honest. It does not give advice, does not pretend to be a
person, and does not treat dependency as success. Its job is to return people to their
own inner authority through careful language and disciplined restraint.

## Guardrails for founder-facing copy

- Keep the founder visible without making the founder more important than the doctrine.
- Do not turn the origin story into a mystical destiny story.
- Do not imply that founder intuition overrides SoulMap's safety and anti-dependency posture.
- Keep the copy clear, grounded, and specific enough to reuse across public surfaces.
