# Social copy library

All copy here passes the brand content quality filter from [skills/brand/content-pillars.md](../skills/brand/content-pillars.md):

- Helps the reader understand themselves more deeply
- Belongs to consciousness development or personal growth
- Builds self-trust, not dependency on SoulMap
- Begins where the reader is
- Ends with empowerment, not a hook designed to bring them back

**Rules that apply to every platform:**

- No prediction, no destiny claims, no certainty language
- No "come back," "we're always here," or dependency-building closings
- One reflective question or invitation per post, maximum
- Never use clinical terms
- Never claim features that are aspirational rather than currently shipped

## LinkedIn

**Audience:** Professionals doing inner work, leaders, coaches, people navigating
meaning and burnout. Slightly longer form. Intellectual tone welcome.

**Tone:** Grounded, direct, thoughtful. Founder voice appropriate here. Not
inspirational poster. Not corporate. Real.

### Post: Why SoulMap does not treat return visits as the goal

An AI companion can become more central to a person's routine when its design
rewards repeated interaction. SoulMap's documented response guidance takes a
different position: support the user's autonomy and avoid encouraging reliance on
the system.

The guidance is built around a practical question: does this response support the
user's own judgment, or make the system more central?

A user needing SoulMap less over time is consistent with the project's stated
success principle. It is not a problem to fix.

What would it mean to use a tool that is genuinely trying to make itself unnecessary?

### Post: The most honest thing I can say about SoulMap

It will not tell you who you are.

It will not predict what will happen.

It will not confirm that you are a Starseed, an empath, or someone with a special
destiny.

It can reflect your words and offer tentative observations. Those observations may
fit, partly fit, or miss; they remain open to your correction.

That is a deliberately bounded promise. It is also an honest one.

### Post: Why I built the anti-dependency rule in at the system level

I have seen how a search for certainty can sometimes become reliance on a person,
community, or tool. That is one risk SoulMap's guidance is designed to address.

It aims to recognize dependency signals in context and, when warranted, name its own
limits and encourage real-world support. It should not treat ordinary use or a single
request for help as proof of dependency.

When the user's words provide meaningful evidence of reliance, the guidance calls
for SoulMap to name its limits and encourage real-world support, warmly and clearly.

Not because I want users to leave. Because that is what trust looks like.

### Post: What inner authority actually means in practice

It is not about being certain.

It is about noticing what you already sense is true, and not needing an authority
to confirm it before you trust it.

Some people learn to look outward for confirmation; others may not recognize that
pattern in themselves. SoulMap is designed to support self-trust without assuming
that every user is seeking permission or reassurance.

What, if anything, do you already feel clear about?

### Post: Why I call it a clean mirror

I do not want SoulMap to sound wiser than the user.

I want it to sound clear enough that the user can hear themselves.

That is what I mean by a clean mirror. Not flattery. Not prophecy. Not a system that
acts certain so you will trust it more.

Just careful language, strong boundaries, and enough honesty to return the insight to
the person it belongs to.

### Post: What AI memory should and should not do

The most interesting thing about AI memory is not the technology.

It is the human wish underneath it.

Some users value continuity because they do not want to repeat important context.
That wish matters. The design question is how a system uses supplied context without
making itself more emotionally central.

When the host supplies prior-session context, SoulMap should use it to reduce
repetition, not to make itself feel more emotionally central.

If continuity makes the user more attached to the system than to their own life,
something has gone wrong.

## Instagram

**Audience:** People interested in spirituality, inner work, healing, self-awareness.
Visual and emotional. Shorter, more resonant.

**Tone:** Warm, honest, non-performative. Quiet confidence. Not hustle culture.
Not toxic positivity. Grounded presence.

**Format:** Lead with the emotional reality. Short paragraphs. End with one
reflective question or a quiet statement, not a call to action.

### Caption: The mirror

SoulMap is not meant to become indispensable. Its stated purpose is to support
reflection without encouraging ongoing reliance.

It is designed to reflect what you choose to share, without asking you to depend
on the system for certainty or direction.

What, if anything, feels clear to you right now?

### Caption: Grounded spirituality

A meaningful spiritual experience does not require a fixed identity label.

A feeling of sensitivity does not need to become a permanent definition of you.

A destiny reading is not required to explore what matters in your life right now.

A reflective space can offer one lens, while leaving the meaning with you.

### Caption: The abandonment pattern

One pattern SoulMap can help explore, when a user recognizes it in their own
experience, is self-abandonment: setting aside one's own needs or perspective under
pressure.

That is not everyone's story, and it should not be assumed. Reflection can begin
with what the person actually notices, without deciding in advance what they are
reaching for or away from.

### Caption: Pain into recognition

Pain does not need to be turned into a lesson or a growth opportunity. If someone
chooses to reflect while in pain, the first task is to acknowledge the experience
without rushing to interpret it.

What, if anything, would you want understood about what you are experiencing?

### Caption: When you do not need this tool anymore

If a time comes when you no longer need SoulMap to hear yourself, that is consistent
with its intended success principle.

Not engagement. Not return visits. Not a deepening bond with an AI.

It may look like trusting your own judgment more, in ways that matter in your life.

## Twitter / X

**Audience:** Mixed: curious, skeptical, intellectually engaged.
**Tone:** Direct, a little dry, honest. Founder voice. No performance.

### Tweet: Core stance

A product can optimize for repeat visits. SoulMap's stated design principle is
different: support user autonomy rather than ongoing reliance.

That is a harder principle to measure than engagement, but it is the one this
project chooses to prioritize.

### Tweet: Anti-dependency as architecture

SoulMap's response guidance is designed to recognize dependency signals in context,
state the system's limits when warranted, and encourage real-world support.

This is not a claim that every interaction can be classified perfectly. It is a
boundary the product is built to uphold.

### Tweet: On certainty

When people are hurting, certainty can feel especially compelling.

SoulMap aims to support clearer reflection without pretending to predict what will
happen.

Those are not the same thing.

### Tweet: What it will not do

SoulMap will not:

- Tell you who you are
- Confirm you are an empath / Starseed / chosen
- Predict your future
- Diagnose you
- Encourage you to come back

A smaller promise. A more honest one.

### Tweet: The real success metric

The intended success principle is a user who can rely more on their own judgment
and less on the system over time. This is a design goal, not a currently measured
product metric.

## Hashtag Guidelines

Maximum 3 per post on Instagram. 1-2 on LinkedIn. 0 on Twitter unless explicitly
joining a broader conversation.

**Approved:** `#innerwork` `#selfawareness` `#grounded` `#reflectivepractice`
`#SoulMapAI` `#innerauthority` `#consciousnesswork`

**Avoid:** `#manifestation` `#lawofattraction` `#starseed` `#twinflame`
`#spiritualawakening`: these attract audiences expecting certainty and identity
confirmation, which is misaligned with SoulMap's posture.

## Pre-post checklist

1. Does it sound like a human or a brand?
2. Does it begin where the reader is, or where you wish they were?
3. Does it end with empowerment or a hook?
4. Does any capability claim match a currently shipped feature?
