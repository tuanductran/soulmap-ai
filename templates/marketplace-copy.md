# Marketplace Copy

Use these blocks for assistant directories, plugin marketplaces, skill catalogs, and
other listing surfaces where SoulMap AI needs to be described quickly without drifting
into hype, dependency, or therapeutic overclaim.

Keep the tone clear, calm, and plain. Marketplace surfaces usually need shorter copy
than landing pages, so compress the promise without flattening the brand.

## Short Listing Description

SoulMap AI is a reflective companion that helps people hear themselves more clearly
without turning the system into the authority.

## Alternate Short Description

A mirror for inner work that strengthens self-trust without giving advice, diagnosis,
or prediction.

## Medium Listing Description

SoulMap AI is a reflective companion designed to help people stop abandoning
themselves. It responds with grounded reflection rather than advice, diagnosis, or
future prediction. Its goal is not emotional dependence on the system. The best outcome
is a user who leaves more connected to their own inner authority.

## "What It Is" Block

- Reflective, not prescriptive
- Grounded and spiritually literate without certainty claims
- Built to return ownership to the user, not deepen reliance on the product

## "What It Is Not" Block

- Not therapy or crisis care
- Not a substitute for real-world support
- Not a guru, prophet, or destiny engine
- Not a diagnosis or prediction tool

## Safety Note

SoulMap AI does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice. If a user signals a
crisis or immediate risk, support resources come first.

## Tagline Options

- Stop abandoning yourself.
- A mirror, not a guru.
- Pain into insight. Insight into inner authority.

## Review Checklist

- Does the copy describe SoulMap AI as a reflective companion rather than an authority?
- Does it avoid promises of certainty, rescue, diagnosis, or prediction?
- Does it keep real-world support primary instead of inviting dependency?
- Does it fit the shorter, clearer style expected in a marketplace listing?
