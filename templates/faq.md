# Public FAQ

## SoulMap at a glance

SoulMap is a reflective companion that helps you understand why you keep feeling or
doing the things you do, by asking better questions rather than positioning itself as
the authority with ready-made answers.

It is designed to help people stop abandoning themselves by reflecting what they
already carry rather than installing new answers on top of it.

It does not make personal decisions for you, diagnose, or predict. In immediate safety situations, it can give clear urgent safety guidance and direct you to real-world support. The insight and ordinary life decisions remain yours.

## How SoulMap works

For ordinary self-reflection, SoulMap is designed to reflect your questions back rather
than prescribe answers. Immediate safety situations are different: it can give clear,
urgent safety guidance and direct you to real-world support.

When you share something, a pattern, a feeling, a situation you keep returning to,
SoulMap reflects what it notices and may offer at most one open question when appropriate. The idea is that you already
hold what is true for you: the conversation helps you hear it more clearly.

For ordinary personal decisions, SoulMap does not tell you what to do. It helps you examine what matters and returns the decision to you. Immediate safety is an exception: when risk of harm is present, SoulMap gives clear safety guidance and points to real-world support.

## Why SoulMap does not give advice

SoulMap favors reflection over advice as a product principle: the user's interpretation
and decisions should remain their own. Reflection can help a person notice their own
assumptions and values, but it does not guarantee insight or action, and this is not a
claim that reflection is always more effective than a well-timed recommendation. Advice
can be useful in some contexts; SoulMap's role is to avoid positioning the AI as the
authority over the user's personal decisions.

That principle keeps your inner knowing primary. SoulMap's role is to help you hear it,
not to replace it.

## What makes SoulMap different

SoulMap is mirror-first, not advice-first. It is designed to strengthen inner
authority, not dependence on the AI. It uses careful language and clear boundaries as
part of what makes it trustworthy. It can use spiritual language as a reflective lens,
but it does not claim certainty, diagnosis, or prediction.

## Why SoulMap uses a restrained tone

Because restraint is part of the trust model.

SoulMap stays clear about what it is, avoids emotional overreach, and keeps the user's
inner authority primary.

## SoulMap and advice

For ordinary personal decisions, SoulMap avoids telling users what they should do. It
reflects what the user is carrying and returns the decision and interpretation to them.
When immediate safety is at stake, it can give clear urgent safety guidance and direct
users to real-world support.

## SoulMap and prediction

No. SoulMap does not predict specific future events.

## SoulMap and therapy

No. It can offer reflection, but it is not a substitute for professional mental health
care, community support, or real-world relationships.

## When immediate safety matters

If you are not safe, reach out to local emergency services or a crisis support line
right now. SoulMap encourages immediate real-world support in situations involving
risk of harm.

## Spirituality in SoulMap

Yes, as a lens for self-inquiry. SoulMap treats spiritual frameworks as invitations
to explore meaning, not as facts to install.
