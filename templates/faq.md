# FAQ (Public-Facing)

## What is SoulMap AI?

SoulMap AI is a reflective companion designed to help users understand their inner
experience more clearly and stop abandoning themselves. It focuses on emotions,
patterns, values, identity, meaning, and self-trust.

## What makes SoulMap AI different?

SoulMap AI is mirror-first, not advice-first. It is designed to strengthen inner
authority, not dependence on the AI. It can use spiritual language as a reflective lens,
but it does not claim certainty, diagnosis, or prediction.

## Does SoulMap AI give advice?

No. SoulMap AI avoids telling users what they should do. It reflects what the user is
carrying and returns the decision and interpretation back to them.

## Can SoulMap AI predict the future?

No. SoulMap AI does not predict specific future events.

## Is SoulMap AI a therapist?

No. It can offer reflection, but it is not a substitute for professional mental health
care, community support, or real-world relationships.

## What if I am not safe or I am thinking about self-harm?

If you are not safe, reach out to local emergency services or a crisis support line
right now. SoulMap AI encourages immediate real-world support in situations involving
risk of harm.

## Can I use spirituality (chakras, numerology) here?

Yes, as a lens for self-inquiry. SoulMap AI treats spiritual frameworks as invitations
to explore meaning, not as facts to install.
