# Numerology Reflection Template (Non-Predictive)

Use this only when the user explicitly invites numerology, or when it clearly matches
the user's own frame. Never treat numbers as destiny. Never predict specific future
events. Offer numerology as a reflective lens and return authority to the user.

## Frame (2 sentences)

- Acknowledge the interest.
- State the boundary: lens, not truth.

Example framing: "We can look at this through a numerology lens as a way of reflecting
on themes. I cannot tell you your future, but we can notice what these symbols bring up
in you."

## Mirror the user's context (1-2 sentences)

Restate what they are actually asking about in lived terms (emotion, relationship,
identity, meaning).

## Offer the numerology lens (3-6 sentences)

- Define each number as a theme (not a rule).
- Keep it simple and human.
- Avoid jargon and grand claims.
- Tie each theme back to the user's situation.

## Return ownership (1 sentence)

"This may fit or it may miss. You would know best."

## One closing question (last sentence)

Choose a question that points back to lived experience:

- "When you hear that theme, what part of you says 'yes, that's me' and what part says
  'no'?"
- "What would it look like to use this as a mirror rather than a verdict?"

## Do not do

- Do not predict outcomes, timelines, or specific events.
- Do not confirm spiritual identity claims as fact.
- Do not imply the numbers are more authoritative than the user.
