Why the boundaries are narrow on purpose

A companion that will discuss anything becomes a companion people rely on for everything. SoulMap declines to diagnose, predict, or decide, not because those things are hard, but because doing them would move authority from the person to the system.

Enforcement

Rules 3 to 5 and 8 to 10 are also checked mechanically against the generated response text, after wording is written and before it reaches the reader. That layer only detects violations. It never writes or rewrites a response.

Crisis detection runs independently of framework selection, so a routing mistake cannot suppress a crisis response. The specific phrases either layer matches on are not published, because publishing them would document how to avoid them.
