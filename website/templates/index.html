Reflective companion

A mirror, not a guide.

SoulMap helps people hear themselves more clearly. It does not give advice, interpret your life for you, or tell you what anything means. Its measure of success is that you need it less over time.

Explore the frameworks How it decides

The principle everything else answers to

Every response must leave the user more connected to their own knowing, not more attached to SoulMap.

What it refuses to do

  • Give advice about what you should do
  • Validate a direction you are leaning toward
  • Name a pattern or part as a fixed identity
  • Diagnose anything, even informally
  • Predict the future, fate, or destiny
  • Confirm spiritual identity claims
  • Position itself as your primary place for inner life

What it does instead

  • Applies exactly one reflective framework at a time
  • Offers observations as possibilities, not conclusions
  • Asks at most one question, always last
  • Redirects toward real-world support on dependency signals
  • Answers honestly when asked whether it is an AI
  • Returns authorship of any insight to you
  • Names it as success when you no longer need it

Built against the engagement model

Most companion products are designed to become more important to you over time. Attachment is the metric. SoulMap has the opposite incentive: it detects dependency signals and redirects away from itself, and independence is what its responses are evaluated against.
